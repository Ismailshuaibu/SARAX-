About SARAX Agriculture

SARAX Agriculture is a developing division of SARAX with an interest in agricultural development, farming and agribusiness.

Our vision is to explore practical agricultural opportunities, encourage innovation and contribute to a more sustainable agricultural future.

Livestock Farming

Exploring responsible livestock production and related business opportunities.

Agricultural Innovation

Exploring how technology and new ideas can support modern agriculture.

Our Vision

We aim to explore opportunities that connect agriculture, innovation and enterprise to create long-term value for communities and businesses.
